Fast is useful until it becomes the only measure.

Some tools ask us to wait, choose, or write something down. That friction can feel old-fashioned, but it gives the mind a place to catch up with the hand.

## The speed of attention

Attention is not a background process. It is the material of the work. A tool that makes every action instant can still make the work feel strangely far away.

The best tool is not always the one that disappears. Sometimes it is the one that reminds us we are here.

## A deliberate pace

Slow tools are not about nostalgia. They are about choosing a pace that keeps the result connected to the person making it.
